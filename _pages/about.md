I am a predoctoral researcher at the MIT Sloan School of Management, where I work with Mert Demirer and Catherine Wolfram on empirical research in industrial organization and environmental economics.

I hold an M.Sc. in Economics from the London School of Economics (2026), on its labor economics track, and an A.B. in Politics from Princeton University (2025), with certificates in Latin American Studies and in Spanish Language and Culture.

My research covers labor economics, contentious politics, and Latin America. My M.Sc. dissertation studies the effects of strikes on neighborhood labor markets in the United States, and my senior thesis studied the success of contentious activity. Before graduate school, I was a research assistant to Ellora Derenoncourt at Princeton's Industrial Relations Section, a research intern at Policy Matters Ohio, and a labor organizer.
