The board ranks accounts on MIT's Engaging cluster by core-hours and by the share of requested cores and memory each account's jobs put to use.

Open the board at [github.mit.edu/pages/baspring/engaging-usage](https://github.mit.edu/pages/baspring/engaging-usage/) (MIT login required).
