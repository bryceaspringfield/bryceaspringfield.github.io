description: "An add-on for Firefox and Chrome which marks sites on the AFL-CIO boycott list."
importance: 3
category: add-ons
---

The Union Boycott Helper shows a toolbar badge and a dismissible banner on the site of a company on the AFL-CIO boycott list, with the entry's source link and source date, and labels matching search results. Every match is computed in the browser, and the add-on sends no request anywhere.
