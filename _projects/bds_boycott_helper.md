The BDS Boycott Helper shows a toolbar badge and a dismissible banner on the site of a company on the BDS movement's consumer boycott, pressure, and divestment lists, with the entry's source link, source date, and alternatives. On nine search engines, the add-on labels or hides results linking to a listed site. Every match is computed in the browser, and the add-on sends no request anywhere.
