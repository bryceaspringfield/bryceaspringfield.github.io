Horizon projects a household's income, spending, accounts, and taxes year by year. It models US federal and state income taxes, estate taxes by state, Roth conversions, required distributions, and Social Security, and it tests a plan with Monte Carlo runs and historical market sequences. Plans stay on the device, or sync end-to-end encrypted behind a username and a password.

Open the app at [bryceaspringfield.github.io/horizon](https://bryceaspringfield.github.io/horizon/).
