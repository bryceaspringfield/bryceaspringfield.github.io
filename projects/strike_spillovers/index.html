Strike Action and Neighborhood Labor Markets

Do US strikes since 2021 change employment, wages, and establishment counts in adjacent counties?

This project asks whether US strikes since 2021 change private-sector employment, wages, and establishment counts in adjacent counties with no strike. The work extends an MSc dissertation at the London School of Economics toward a journal submission. The paper is listed under publications.
